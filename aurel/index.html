About the studio

One chair. One clinician.
The whole way through.

AUREL was opened in 2011 by four specialists who were tired of handing patients between rooms. Whoever plans your treatment performs it, reviews it, and sees you at the follow-up.

The studio occupies one floor in Gate Village. There are four surgeries, a scanning room and a ceramics bench — which is unusual, and is the reason a veneer case that would normally take three weeks takes eight days here.
