About

A restaurant with one idea
and no interest in a second.

01

It started as a mistake

In 2016 we over-ordered prawns by roughly forty kilos and spent a week cooking our way out of it, giving them away to anyone who came through the door. By the Friday there was a queue. We took the rest of the menu down the following Monday and have never properly put it back.

02

Everything is about the turn

A raw prawn is grey-blue and translucent. Ninety seconds later it is coral, opaque and curled, and thirty seconds after that it is ruined. The whole restaurant is arranged around that window — the pans, the pass, where the tables are, how many we take at once.

03

We are not a quiet room

There are no tablecloths, the bowls are for the shells, and everyone gets a cloth because everyone needs one. Booking is for two hours. That is long enough for three courses and not long enough for a meeting.

“The party is the point. The prawns are just how we get people to it.” — Nadia Okonjo, who did the over-ordering
