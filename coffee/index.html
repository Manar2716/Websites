The film, in words

One bean

Eleven months on the branch and eleven minutes in the drum. A roasted seed is an ellipsoid with one flat face and a crease down the middle of it, and under a single hard light that crease catches and loses the highlight once per turn. That is the whole first shot.

Picked nine times

Cherry ripens unevenly, so the slope is walked nine times across eleven weeks and only the ripe fruit comes off. Stripping a branch in one pass is faster and it is why most coffee tastes like most coffee.

Sixty grams

Every cup is thrown to one profile, with a wall that thickens toward the base. Heat leaves it slower than it leaves your hands, which is the only measurement in ceramics that a drinker actually notices.

Twenty-five seconds

Nine bars at ninety-three degrees through eighteen grams. The crema arrives tiger-striped, holds for about four minutes, and is the first thing to tell you whether the grind was right.

Sixty-two degrees

Milk is steamed to sixty-two and not one degree past it. Beyond that the lactose stops reading as sweetness and the whole cup turns flat and slightly cooked. The rosetta is drawn in one pass and pulled through at the end.

Two hundred parts

A lever group, a copper boiler, a burr set trued to two microns, and about two hundred machined parts around them. Four people assemble it in one room in Turin, and it is designed to be taken apart by whoever owns it.
