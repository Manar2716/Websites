Three cameras.
All 48 megapixels.

Main. Ultra Wide. Telephoto.

Main 48 MP The camera you shoot with almost all of the time.
Ultra Wide 48 MP Steps back without you stepping back. The whole room, the whole building.
Telephoto 48 MP Reaches across a room or a street, with glass rather than by enlarging the middle of the picture.
Main camera aperture ƒ/1.48 – ƒ/4.0 The opening behind the lens changes size. Wide open it lets in the most light; closed down it lets in less and holds more of the picture sharp.

Variable aperture
More control over light. ƒ/1.48 to ƒ/4.0. Open for the dark, closed for the bright.

Outdoor brightness 3000 nits Bright enough to stay readable in direct sun — which is the only place you ever notice it.
Chip A20 Pro
20% faster CPU Six cores. Apps open and stay open; they aren’t quietly thrown out of memory while you are somewhere else.
40% faster GPU Seven cores. Games hold their frame rate instead of sagging after ten minutes.
It develops every photo. Each time you press the shutter the chip merges several exposures into one picture — before you have lifted your thumb.

Apple’s published specifications

  • Display 6.9″ OLED Super Retina XDR · ProMotion to 120 Hz · up to 3000 nits outdoor brightness.
  • Chip A20 Pro. Software iOS 27, with Apple Intelligence.
  • From Apple’s own comparison against iPhone 17 Pro Max: up to 6 more hours video playback, up to 20% faster 6-core CPU, up to 40% faster 7-core GPU.
  • Cameras 48 MP Main, 48 MP Ultra Wide, 48 MP Telephoto. Main aperture variable, ƒ/1.48–ƒ/4.0. Front camera 18 MP.
  • Battery up to 45 hours video playback.
  • Storage 256 GB · 512 GB · 1 TB · 2 TB.
  • Connectivity USB‑C · Wi‑Fi 7 · Bluetooth 6 · 5G.
  • Build aluminium unibody, IP68, approximately 249 g.
  • Finishes Black, Silver, Glacier, Burgundy.

Derived on this deck, not quoted from Apple

  • The hour-by-hour percentages on the battery scene and the day scene are an illustration of a day’s shape, not a measurement. Real battery life depends on signal, brightness, temperature and what you actually run. The 45-hour figure is a video-playback rating and is described as one.
  • The camera scene’s six situations are abstract light studies, not photographs taken on this or any phone.

On the photography

  • Every device on screen is real product photography, cut from three captures of Apple’s iPhone 18 Pro pages by tools/build-assets.py. Nothing is drawn and nothing is AI-generated.
  • Four views: the front and the rear three-quarter, the camera macro, and all four finishes. One device in one finish carries the run, so it reads as a continuous look at a single object.
  • Two earlier sources were dropped. They showed a square camera island with the lenses in a triangle — 15/16 Pro geometry, not this generation’s full-width plateau — so they were a render of a different design, and nothing from them survives here.
  • Which lens is which follows Apple’s conventional Pro layout: Main top-left, Ultra Wide below it, Telephoto centre. The call-out rings are positioned from measurements taken on the photograph itself.

Sourcing note

apple.com, support.apple.com and every Apple CDN were unreachable from the machine this was built on. The product photography came from screen captures of Apple’s pages supplied directly; the written specifications were cross-checked against reporting that quotes Apple’s specification pages. The CPU, GPU and battery comparison figures are read from Apple’s own comparison module in one of those captures. Confirm everything against Apple’s tech-spec page before presenting.
