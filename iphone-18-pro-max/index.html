Apple’s published specifications

  • 45 hours video playback, 40 hours streamed video — iPhone 18 Pro Max. Apple describes it as the longest battery life in an iPhone.
  • 39 hours video playback, 35 hours streamed — iPhone 17 Pro Max, the generation before.
  • Main camera 48 MP Fusion, 24 mm, variable aperture ƒ/1.48 · ƒ/1.8 · ƒ/2.8 · ƒ/4, six blades, second-generation sensor-shift stabilisation.
  • Ultra Wide 48 MP, 13 mm, ƒ/2.2, 120° field of view.
  • Telephoto 48 MP, 100 mm (4×), ƒ/2.8, tetraprism, plus a 12 MP optical-quality 8× at 200 mm.
  • Front 18 MP Centre Stage.
  • Display 6.9″, 1000 nits typical, 1600 nits peak HDR, 3000 nits peak outdoor.
  • A20 Pro, Apple’s first 2 nm chip.
  • Charging to 50% in around 15 minutes with a 60W-capable USB-PD 3.1 adapter; the prior generation was around 20 minutes.
  • Manual controls for aperture, shutter speed and white balance, with a histogram. Dual Capture records front and back at once.
  • Ceramic Shield 2; smaller Dynamic Island holding up to three Live Activities.

Apple UAE pricing

  • 256 GB — AED 5,499 · 512 GB — AED 6,349 · 1 TB — AED 8,049 · 2 TB — AED 10,599
  • Four finishes: black, silver, glacier, burgundy. Pre-orders 12 September 2026; on sale 18 September 2026.

Derived on this deck, not quoted from Apple

  • “Six more hours” and “roughly fifteen percent” are arithmetic on Apple’s own 45 h and 39 h ratings (45 − 39 = 6; 6 ÷ 39 ≈ 15.4%).
  • Opening to ƒ/1.48 from ƒ/1.78 is about 1.45× the light — the ratio of the apertures squared. Apple publishes the aperture values; the multiplier is ours.
  • Slide 9’s hour-by-hour percentages are an illustration of a day, not a measurement. Real battery life depends on signal, brightness, temperature and what you actually run.

What this deck deliberately does not claim

  • No battery capacity in mAh. Apple does not publish it and third-party teardown figures disagree with each other, so only Apple’s hour ratings appear here.
  • No new camera sensors. The three 48 MP sensors carry over; the change on the main camera is the physically moving aperture, not the sensor. Slide 6 says so on the slide.
  • Battery bars start at zero. 39 h is drawn at 86.7% of 45 h. Cropping the axis would have made the gap look bigger than it is.
  • Independent reviewers measured less improvement under sustained gaming than the video-playback rating suggests. The rating is a video-playback rating and is described as one.

Imagery

  • There is no photograph and no image file in this project. The device, the lenses and all six photography scenes are drawn in code from gradients and canvas paths. The photography scenes illustrate a lighting situation; they are not camera samples, and the slide says so while they are on screen.
