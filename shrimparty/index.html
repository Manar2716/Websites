01 — THE HOUSE

We boil by the bucket because a plate is the wrong shape for this food.

Everything arrives in a galvanised pail, still steaming, still wearing the boil. There is no cutlery on the table and no plate under the pail. You tip it out, you use your hands, and the paper on the table is there to be ruined.

The spice is built in three stages over eleven hours — a dry blend toasted the night before, a stock that runs from morning service, and a butter finished to order. Ask for it mild and we will make it mild. Ask for it the way the kitchen eats it and you should mean it.

  • 34spices in the blend
  • 4heat levels
  • 180seats on the water
