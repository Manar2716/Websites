BEFORE YOU COME

THINGS PEOPLE ASK

Is there cutlery?

There are crackers and picks for the crab, and that is deliberate. The buckets are eaten with your hands. If you would rather not, the plates section of the menu is for you and nobody will think anything of it.

How hot is Carnival, honestly?

Hot enough that the staff will confirm it twice, and hot enough that we bring the salted watermelon without being asked. Cajun is where most people who think they want Carnival should stop.

Can you do a bucket without shellfish?

Not honestly. The stock is shells. There is a coal-roast corn, the broccolini, the sides and the sweet, but a table with a shellfish allergy is a table we would rather warn than disappoint.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, and there is usually a wait after 20:00. The bar takes walk-ins first and the pails come out just as fast there.

Is it loud?

Yes. It is a room full of people cracking crab. The terrace is quieter and the water helps.

Children?

Very welcome, and mild is genuinely mild. The hush puppies have never once come back.
