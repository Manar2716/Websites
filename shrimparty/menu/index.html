THE BOARD

THE WHOLE BOARD

Priced by the pail and by the plate. Every bucket is boiled to order, which is why it takes twenty-five minutes and why it is worth it.

Prices in AED · service included · kitchen closes 00:30

01 — THE BUCKETS

The Buckets

Thirty litres of stock, a wire basket, and a pail turned out onto paper. Nothing here is served with cutlery.

  • The Seafood Bucket SERVES 2–3

    395

    Snow crab, tiger prawn, black mussel, andouille, corn, new potato.

  • The Shrimp Bucket SERVES 2

    265

    A kilo of head-on Gulf prawn in garlic butter, cayenne and lemon.

  • The Crab Bucket SERVES 2

    445

    Snow crab clusters only, cracked at the pass, drawn butter on the side.

  • The Whole Damn Thing SERVES 5–6

    1,180

    Both buckets, a lobster, and every side on this page. Ordered once per table.

From the Coals

  • Grilled Salmon

    145

    Skin down, blackened on one side only. Charred lemon, dill butter, burnt scallion.

  • Whole Sea Bream SERVES 2

    185

    Scored, salted, chermoula through the cuts. Served on the board it was cooked on.

  • Tiger Prawn Skewer

    110

    Six, head on, over the coals for ninety seconds a side.

  • Coal-Roast Corn

    38

    Rolled in the boil butter and cayenne while it is still too hot to hold.

05 — THE HEAT

The Heat

Four levels. The blend is the same in all of them; what changes is how much of it and which chillies are in the butter.

  • Mild

    no charge

    The blend, no chilli in the butter. Still tastes of something.

  • Medium

    no charge

    Cayenne. Where most of the room lands, and where we would start.

  • Cajun

    no charge

    Cayenne and bird’s eye. You will notice it at the back of your throat.

  • Carnival SIGN NOTHING

    no charge

    Both, plus a long pepper that arrives late and does not leave.
